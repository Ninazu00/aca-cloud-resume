  • Friday is fully off. It is not part of the week, not counted as free time, and only for genuine emergencies.

Notes

AWS Certified Security – Specialty (SCS-C03)

The current exam version since 2 Dec 2025: 65 questions (50 scored) in 170 minutes, pass mark 750 of 1000, $300 a sitting. Domains: IAM 20%, Infrastructure Security 18%, Data Protection 18%, Detection 16%, Incident Response 14%, Security Foundations and Governance 14%.

Courses: Stephane Maarek's Ultimate Solutions Architect Associate (SAA-C03) for foundations, then his Ultimate AWS Certified Security Specialty (SCS-C03), then Tutorials Dojo SCS-C03 practice exams. The full week-by-week watch list is in the separate AWS guide.

Hours: about 153h planned. 81h by 10 Dec, about 4h of optional flashcards across the exam weeks, 25h in the mid-year break, 24h across the four Ramadan weeks at 6h, then 9h in the week of 13 Mar and 10h in the reading week before the exam. The 6h minimum alone is not enough; the extra hours are what keep 23 Mar alive.

Where the hours go: Tuesday and Thursday are the two open days and carry the study, about 3h each. Saturday, Sunday and Monday add about 1h each after class. Nothing on Wednesday. That is 9h; the 6h minimum is just Tuesday and Thursday.

Cost: new AWS accounts get a Free plan that closes after 6 months or when credits run out. Organizations and IAM Identity Center need the Paid plan. Do not build Private CA, CloudHSM, Network Firewall, Verified Access or Security Lake; watch those demos only.
