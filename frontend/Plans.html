Free time

The weekly pool is what is left of 144 hours after everything that is fixed. Free time each week is the pool minus that week’s planned hours.

  • Hours in a Saturday to Thursday week144h
  • less Sleep (7.5h x 6 nights)45h
  • less Meals and personal admin (2h x 6)12h
  • less PPL, piano and Calibration7.5h
  • less Class blocks19h
  • less Commute (2h x 5 campus days)10h
  • less On-campus gaps (lost time, not free)7h
  • Weekly pool, normal teaching week43.5h

Reading Week and Revision Week use a 70h pool. That figure is not derived from the timetable; if Reading Week has no classes at all, the real pool is closer to 79h.

Notes

CCNP SCOR reality check

Cisco's recommended profile for SCOR (350-701) is 3–5 years implementing security solutions. That is guidance, not a gate, and plenty of strong CCNA-level students pass without a security job behind them.

By S2 you will have a semester of security-adjacent Linux and scripting plus real network-security exposure from your internship. That helps on concepts but not on the Cisco product knowledge SCOR leans on (ISE, Firepower, Umbrella, AMP for Endpoints, Cisco Secure Email), which has no overlap with FortiGate or app-security pentesting.

Budget about 110–150 hours, with hands-on practice (GNS3, CML or pnetlab, Cisco dCloud, or a practice-exam engine like Boson ExSim) on top of the CBT Nuggets videos, not instead of them.

Spread over mid-March to early July (about 15–16 weeks) that is 7–9h a week on average, leaning on June and July. Treat it as softer than the S1 numbers and re-read it after two weeks of real CBT Nuggets material.

Keep Firewall and SNCF as bonus goals. Only start them if SCOR is done with room to spare, say by mid-May.
